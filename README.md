# Data Ethics and Disability

The ethical use of disabled customer data.

## Overview

This repository brings together guidance, research framing, and governance principles for using data related to disabled people in a fair, privacy-preserving, and dignity-respecting way. It focuses on accessibility, inclusion, and ethical decision-making in product, service, and AI-related contexts.

The material is intended to support organisations that need to understand accessibility barriers and user needs without collecting, profiling, or monitoring disabled people in ways that compromise autonomy, trust, or human rights.

## Repository contents

- `Ethical and Inclusive Use of Data Relating to Disabled People.md` — a practical framework covering ethical principles, data collection boundaries, accessibility signals, governance checks, and guidance for AI and data use.
- `CUSTOMER RESEARCH FRAMEWORK.md` — a structured framework for identifying customer segments, personas, pain points, journeys, decision criteria, and accessibility-related needs from repository material or broader organisational intelligence.
- `LICENSE` — MIT License.

## Core themes

- Nothing about us without us.
- Equivalence of experience over narrow task completion.
- Dignity as an accessibility requirement.
- Privacy by design and minimum necessary data collection.
- Aggregated insight instead of individual profiling.
- Accessibility evidence from diverse user groups.
- Governance and transparency in AI, analytics, and service design.

## Intended use

This repository is a policy and research resource for:

- accessibility and inclusion teams
- product and service designers
- data governance and privacy professionals
- AI procurement and governance stakeholders
- researchers working on ethically responsible data practices
