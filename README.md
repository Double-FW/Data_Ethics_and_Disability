# Data Ethics and Disability

The ethical use of disabled customer data.

## What this repository covers

This repository brings together practical guidance for using data related to disabled people in a fair, privacy-preserving, and dignity-respecting way. It is designed to support organisations that need accessibility insight without turning disabled people into surveillance subjects or data profiles.

It includes:

- `Ethical and Inclusive Use of Data Relating to Disabled People.md` — core principles for dignity, privacy, minimum necessary data, equivalence, and governance.
- `Nothing_About_Us_Without_Us.md` — how to include disabled and neurodivergent people in ordinary research and decision-making.
- `Assistive_Technology_Data_Flows_Risks_and_Review.md` — review patterns for assistive technology, AI tools, third-party scripts, forms, and other data flows.
- `CUSTOMER RESEARCH FRAMEWORK.md` — a structured framework for research, personas, journeys, pain points, and insight capture.

## Processes this repo can support

This guidance can inform and strengthen:

- accessibility and inclusion reviews
- customer and user research design
- AI procurement and governance checks
- HR and employee-facing process review
- product and service design decisions
- data governance and privacy reviews
- vendor and tool assessment for assistive technology or accessibility features
- policy and operating model design for equitable digital experiences

## Core principles

- Nothing about us without us.
- Equivalence of experience over narrow task completion.
- Dignity as an accessibility requirement.
- Minimum necessary data and privacy by design.
- Aggregate insight instead of individual profiling.
- Human accountability and evidence-based decisions.
